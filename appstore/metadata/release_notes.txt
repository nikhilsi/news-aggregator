New in 2.1:

• Today: a short brief of about ten stories, refreshed every hour. Tap "Covered by N outlets" to see how other outlets reported the same story.
• Morning edition: Today's top story once a day, at the time you pick (Settings, Notifications).
• Breaking news: one alert when many outlets cover a big story at once. Off until you turn it on.
• A cleaner reader with styled captions and tables and pinch to zoom. When an article can't open, it says why.
